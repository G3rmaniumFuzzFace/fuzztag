📖 Guide: what this tool does and how to use it

A notepad for writing prompts made of Danbooru tags (for ANIMA, Illustrious and similar models). It suggests real tags while you type, cleans the formatting, and lets you change weight, color, length and order of every tag.

4Copypaste into your image generator

✍️ Typing tags

Type 2 or more letters
Real Danbooru tags appear with the number of images using each one. Tags that start with your letters come first, “alias of …” matches last.

Esc

, comma
Finishes the tag: underscores become spaces, ( ) become \( \), and duplicates are removed. Pasting a whole prompt is cleaned the same way. Face tags like ^_^ and o_o keep their underscores, and :( and :) are fine.

⚖️ Changing weight

Weight 1 is normal and is written plain. Any other weight is written like (blue eyes:1.2). Range: 0 to 10.

Wheel over a block
±0.05 per notch.
Shift + Wheel over a block
±1 per notch (big step).
Ctrl + ↑ / ↓ in the tag box
±0.05 for the tag at the text cursor. (Cmd on Mac.)
Ctrl + Shift + ↑ / ↓
Same, but ±1.
Right-click a block
Opens the block menu (see below) with the −1 / −0.05 / +0.05 / +1 buttons and presets.

↕️ Moving, deleting and selecting blocks

Drag a block
It lifts and follows the mouse. A dashed gap shows where it will land and the other blocks slide aside. Release to drop, Esc cancels.
Click × on a block
Deletes it.
Select button, then click blocks
Pick several blocks and change them together (see “Select several blocks” below).

⚖️ Changing weight

This is where the computer's wheel and Shift shortcuts live on a touch screen: tap a block to open its menu.

Tap a block
The block menu slides up from the bottom (example below). Tap outside it or press ✕ to close.
Weight buttons
−1 and +1 are the big steps. −0.05 and +0.05 are the small steps: press and hold to keep going. The preset row jumps straight to a value (0.5 to 2, or 1 to reset).
Select, then tap blocks
Changes the weight of several blocks at once (±0.05 or back to 1).

🎛️ The block menu

Right-click a block on a computer, or tap it on a touch screen. Example for blue hair:

Most rows (colors too) have a + on the right side of each option, example: posture on sitting. Tapping the name replaces the tag, tapping + adds that option as an extra tag, so you can keep two colors like red hair, blue hair. A ✓ marks the current tag or an extra tag you added: tap it to undo or remove.

Weight
Four step buttons and presets. Weights above 1.6 show a short yellow note (they might distort on Illustrious); it is only a note.
Color
Swaps the color (blue hair → pink hair) or adds one in front of a plain tag (skirt → black skirt).
Length, style and other swap rows
Only appear when the tag has real alternatives: hair length, sleeves, skirt length, smile, posture, angle, clothing states, accessories, and many more. Tap an option to replace the tag (the weight is kept), tap the highlighted one again to undo. Options with no posts on Danbooru are hidden.
See also and Related
Folded rows on every block. Tap a title to open it: tags from the tag's wiki page, or tags often used with it. Tap a tag to add it next to the block. Highlighted ones are already in your prompt: tap again to remove them.
Sort group
A drop-down for fixing a tag the app put in the wrong group. Choose Auto to undo it. Remembered on this device and saved in Save .json.
Guess this tag
Only on gray or weak-guess blocks. See “Groups and sorting”.
Buttons at the bottom
Danbooru wiki, Mute, Lock, Quick-fill (characters only) and Delete. The name with ✕ stays at the top and only the middle scrolls.
Closing it
The menu stays open while you work in it. Close it with ✕, by tapping or clicking outside, or with Esc.

🧰 Mute, Lock and Select several blocks

Mute
Keeps the block but leaves it out of the final prompt (faded and crossed out).
Lock 🔒
Keeps the block in its place when you press Sort.
Select (above the blocks)
Press it, tap or click blocks to pick them, then use the bar: All, − / + (weight ±0.05), Weight 1, Mute, Lock, Move to end, Delete, Clear. Press Done to leave.
Quick-fill
In a character block's menu. Adds the series plus the usual hair, eyes, breast size and outfit tags from Danbooru. Remove what you do not want.

🎨 Groups and sorting

Orders tags: meta, background, series, count, character, hair, eyes and face, body, headwear, accessories, costume, top, bottom, underwear, legwear and shoes, pose and action, props and toys, memes, then uncategorized. Tags in the same group keep your order. Locked blocks stay where they are.
Fixes formatting and removes duplicates without changing the order.
Checkboxes
sort when leaving the box sorts when you click away from the text box. add background if missing adds simple background and the color you pick to the final prompt only.
Reset weights sets every weight back to 1. Clear removes all tags (Undo brings them back).
Color key
Under the blocks. Shows how many blocks each group has. Click a group name to highlight its blocks, click again to turn it off.
Researches every gray tag on Danbooru (parent tags, wiki links, wiki text, related tags). A confident guess keeps its normal color. A weak guess has a dashed border, so check it. Nothing is guessed until you press this.
Reset buttons
Reset manual groups to Auto undoes every group you set by hand. Clear weak guesses forgets only the weak guesses. Both ask first.

🔗 Danbooru suggestions

Blue suggestion box
Appears under the blocks when Danbooru knows something useful. Replace: you typed an alias that is not a real tag, so it offers the real one and keeps your weight. Add: you added a character, so it offers the matching series tag.
Ideas after your last tag
A folded line in the blue box lists tags often used with your last tag. Tap a title to open it, tap a tag to add it, tap it again to remove it.
Sync pill (top of the page)
Says whether Danbooru is reachable. If it says not synced, tap it to retry. Offline, you still get a small built-in tag list.

📋 Output, history and saving

Natural language box
Your sentence is added to the final prompt. order chooses whether tags or the sentence come first.
Final prompt and Copy prompt
Copies exactly the text shown. The counter shows tags and characters. layout chooses grouped lines (quality and background / count and series / character and looks / outfit / pose) or one line.
copy as
Copies a variant: with underscores, without weights, or on one line.
History
Restores earlier prompts. Saved a few seconds after you stop editing, and when you copy.
Save .txt / Save .json / Load file
Download the prompt as text, or everything (tags, sentence, negative, snippets) as a file. Load it back later or on another device.
Undo and Redo
Buttons under the tag box (also Ctrl + Z / Y on a keyboard).
Negative prompt
Its own box with its own Copy button.
Snippets
Save the current tags under a name (an outfit, a character). Click the name later to insert them, × removes it.
Autosave
Your text, snippets, mutes, locks, group choices and block size are kept in this browser.
Warnings
Red lines under the blocks: duplicates, more than one hair or eye color, weight 0, unknown tags, and clashing pairs such as short hair + long hair, 1girl + 2girls, barefoot + socks or sitting + standing.
